Alfredo Pasta


Original Alfredo Pasta as made by the traditional italians

This homemade Alfredo sauce is made with milk, Parmesan cheese, and garlic for a quick and easy pasta sauce that your family will love! You can easily add anything you like, such as grilled shrimp, chicken,
or sautéed peppers.

Ingredients

  • Fettucine pasta
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Butter
  • cream

Directions

  1. Boil Water add salt and oil and let pasta boil till al Dente
  2. Once Pasta is boiled in a Pan heat butter on low flame and add pasta along with boiling pasta water
  3. Add parmesan to it and stir well add cream as per requirement and stir till saucy enough
  4. Voila! your pasta is ready
